# Revision prompts

Built-in imagegen edits of the original PNG mocks.

## Gallery

Remove the word Selected from the selected printing badge. Keep a small checkmark indicator and size the badge to fit. Keep the selection outline. Preserve the rest of the mock.

## Side panel

Remove the Selected badge from the second printing row. Keep the circular checkmark at the left of the row and the selection outline. Keep the area below Borderless clear. Preserve the rest of the mock.
